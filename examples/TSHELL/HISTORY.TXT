TANDY START PUBLICATION HISTORY

TSHELL.EXE and TSINPUT.DLL beside the current source are the 06 candidate.
README.MD, UPDATE06.TXT and TESTS.MD describe this candidate and its limits.

The repository's MILESTONE.ZIP is a preserved earlier standalone milestone,
not the current 06 build. Existing MANIFEST.JSON and EVIDENCE files identify
that earlier publication and remain historical unless explicitly labeled
otherwise. Do not extract the old archive over a current installation or
use old hashes to identify the new executables. No historical file is removed.

The unchanged DOS helper sources/batches and base TSHELL.INI are retained.
Merge ADDON.INI into an installed INI; do not replace existing sound/startup
settings or the working launcher's configured reservation path.
