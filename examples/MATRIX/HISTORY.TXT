MATRIX PUBLICATION HISTORY

Current MATRIX.EXE and SOURCE/MATRIX.C include the /S companion mode for
Tandy Start 06. No-argument on-demand operation remains available.
README.TXT and TESTS.MD identify the current candidate and measured limits.

MILESTONE.ZIP is the preserved older on-demand package. The earlier
MANIFEST.JSN and existing EVIDENCE files describe that historical package,
not current /S behavior. They are retained unchanged. The historical
SOURCE/VERIFY.PY supports the old ordinary /C and /Q fixture layout.

Do not extract the old archive over the current runtime or infer that
on-demand hardware acceptance qualifies the new saver integration.
