CURRENT MATRIX /S RESULTS

The current candidate is identified in TESTS.MD. It passed an independent
byte-identical rebuild, 75 native /S control/restoration cases across five
modes, ordinary-animation regression, duplicate/capture guards and portable
rendering tests. Final TSHELL integration exercised genuine SDL keyboard and
mouse input at 160 and 640 pixels; see ../TSHELL/TESTS.MD for exact scope.

Read TESTS.MD before interpreting these results: four trial modes used a
sample grid rather than complete pixel comparison. Accelerated emulation,
scoped instruction audits and previous on-demand hardware acceptance do not
certify physical /S timing, arbitrary applications or every pixel path.

Existing EVIDENCE files, MANIFEST.JSN and MILESTONE.ZIP remain historical
on-demand records. They have not been relabeled as current /S evidence.
