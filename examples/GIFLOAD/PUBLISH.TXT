GIFLOAD publication layout

The editable source is directly in examples/GIFLOAD, matching README.TXT's
repository-root BUILD.PY command. The native GIFLOAD.EXE, fixtures, BMP
samples, screenshots and complete sealed evidence are in the same subtree.
MILESTONE.ZIP preserves the complete, unchanged standalone GIFLOAD/ package,
including its SOURCE/ folder. SHA256.JSON describes those original paths.
EVIDENCE.JSON's no-publication limit records the pre-publication snapshot.
No compiler, Windows, DDK, guest image or PBRUSH binary is included.

This is a bounded GIF-to-BMP companion for unmodified Paintbrush. The
accepted scope is one opaque full-canvas GIF up to 320x200 and 256 KiB;
transparency, animation and other documented unsupported forms are refused.
Existing output files are protected. Paintbrush format lists are unchanged.
The 243-case suite passed on all five native emulated display profiles
(1,215 native cases), plus seven Paintbrush open/edit/save/reopen workflows.
These are automated Windows-message tests, not manual CUA or hardware tests.
