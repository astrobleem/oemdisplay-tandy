On a problem: stop that test. Note the app, display mode, last action,
exact error and whether keys/mouse still respond. Photograph the full
screen, then a close-up of bad text/colors. A short video helps for
slowness, mouse redraw or sound timing. Avoid a reset while CF activity
is visible. After a forced shutdown, restart from the known-good setup
before testing another app. Do not change several drivers at once.
