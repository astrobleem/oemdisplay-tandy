WINDOWS XT XR1 APPLICATIONS
Programs opens the supplied Maze, Starfield, Matrix, Pinball and About apps.
The shell reads existing Program Manager groups and preserves custom settings.
The separate Minesweeper preview ZIP includes its converted app and About shim;
follow that ZIP's README.TXT to add it. It is not installed by SETUPXT.
Existing experimental app and sound packages retain their own guides.
