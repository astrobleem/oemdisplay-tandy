Sound ordering
The old actual handlers queue WRONG before synchronous next(). On a next guess
REVEAL replaces it before the first note. On a next question simulated work of
650 or 5000 ms expires the 300 ms initial deadline and no note plays. CUE09.PY
compiles both pinned old and new handlers with actual ENGINE.C,DB.C,TDSOUND.C.
New immediate guesses keep REVEAL and skip WRONG; new questions queue WRONG
AFTER computation and refresh, using a fresh timestamp. No fake wait was added.
Both branches pass for work of 0/650/5000 ms, sound on/user off/unsupported:
18 old and 18 new cases. Off/unsupported write no PSG bytes. Enabled cases start
one correct cue and emit its first exact PSG note without lateness. Input guard
stays 220 ms. The sound module itself, ownership checks, lateness drop policy,
focus/close cleanup and cooperative writer lease are unchanged; its 59 existing
host lifecycle/timing/ownership scenarios pass. No physical audio claim.

Leading candidates
Leading: N/M means N of M unrejected names lie within 24 score points of the
current leader. This is the engine's actual score window, not its at-most-64
sample size and not a probability. Outside names stay eligible. Only explicit
rejection removes a name from eligibility. Counts may rise. In the standard
Luigi conflicting-answer case the leading count rises from 93 to 151 on answer
6 while all 256 remain eligible. All 256 truthful target paths were traversed through every explicit rejection
and reset; eligible counts reach zero then reset to 256, and 4118 score-window
rises after rejection demonstrate the distinction. Whole-roster exploratory
cases show 448 count
rises (standard) and 483 (AUDIT08). No elimination or monotonic-count animation.

How I knew
F4/button on guess or success opens all accepted answers, ranked by absolute
signed contribution difference against the actual current runner-up. Stable
ranking preserves answer order for ties. Each page shows the question, user
answer, both stored DAT values, each signed score change, the clue's signed
difference and total margin. Negative and neutral/UNKNOWN clues are retained.
UNKNOWN user answers/facts score zero. Probably values remain labelled Probably.
The page says Not proof. A margin never claims a name was ruled out. If the
history or comparison is incomplete/inconsistent or no competitor remains,
the UI says no complete comparison is available. Every clue and both total
scores are checked against actual C-engine scores. History is bounded by the
engine's full TD_MAXQ=256 unique questions, not the 25-question guessing cap.
130-answer real-DAT and 256-answer boundary histories pass; duplicates, invalid
answers, corruption and overflow are rejected without inventing evidence.
