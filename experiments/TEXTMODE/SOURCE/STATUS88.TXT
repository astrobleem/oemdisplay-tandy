DEFECT AND LIMITS
-----------------
Candidate04 lacked DIB capabilities; its documented GDI monochrome fallback
lost saved color and reopening produced stripes. Candidate05 adds memory
DIB converters and a shadow-only screen bridge. BI_RGB 1/4/8/24 supported;
RLE/othercompression rejected. See DIB88.MD for header/dimension/band limits.
No physical Tandy hardware or cycle-exact bus timing has been tested.
No performance optimization included. Paintbrush repaint interactions can
still take 10-20seconds at fixed 20000 emulator cycles. The isolated converter
benchmark below is not an end-to-end GUI speed or physical8088 estimate.

geometry was changed to make the corrected oracle pass.
