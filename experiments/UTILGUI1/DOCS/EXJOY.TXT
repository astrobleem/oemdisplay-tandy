EXJOY LABEL POLISH TRIAL
The normal header says EXJOY Live, EXJOY Paused, or EXJOY Demo. Paused takes
precedence; a paused demo remains labelled Demo. C reports how many of four axes supplied valid
center timing; zero still says No center timing. A partial center does not
claim joystick presence. Polling, calibration, 4 Hz cadence, I/O and timeout
semantics are unchanged. /demo does no hardware I/O; /test and /porttest
retain diagnostics. See the included EXJOY README.MD for normal controls.
